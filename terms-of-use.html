Your library

The app can change your Zotero library and your WebDAV server: it uploads notes you edit and attachments you annotate, replacing the earlier file. It checks that nobody else changed a file before replacing it, and can keep a backup of files it replaces on WebDAV, but Zotero itself keeps no history of attachment files. Keep your own backups of anything you cannot afford to lose.
