Privacy Policy

Zoot Suit is an Android client for Zotero libraries, a modified version of Zoo for Zotero by Michael Johnston. It is an independent project with no relation to Zotero or the Corporation for Digital Scholarship.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Zoot Suit has no analytics, no advertising and no account with its developer. Crash reporting is switched off in this version; if a later version offers it, sending a report will always be your choice, and this policy will say what a report contains.

Where your data goes

The app talks only to the services you use it with:

  • Zotero (www.zotero.org for sign-in, api.zotero.org for your library). Attachments in Zotero File Storage are downloaded from and uploaded to Zotero's storage, which Zotero hosts with Amazon S3. See Zotero's privacy policy.
  • Your WebDAV server, if you set one up for attachments.
  • The app you open an attachment with (a PDF viewer, for example) receives that file from Zoot Suit.

What is stored on your device

Your Zotero login (an API key), your library data and your settings, including your WebDAV address, username and password, are in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Downloaded attachments are in the app's folder on shared storage (on Android 10 and older, apps with storage permission can read it) or in a folder you chose. Settings → Sign out deletes all of it, apart from files in a folder you chose; uninstalling the app deletes everything but that folder too.

Changes you make

Notes you create or edit are saved to your Zotero library straight away. Attachments you annotate on this device are uploaded to Zotero or your WebDAV server when you sync.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/jimmerricks/zoot-suit.
